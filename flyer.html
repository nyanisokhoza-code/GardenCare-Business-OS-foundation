Lawn Restoration & Garden Maintenance

Hi, thank you for your interest in our garden services. We offer two main services depending on what your lawn and garden require.

Healthy lawns. Beautiful gardens.
If your lawn is tired, patchy, weak or in need of revitalisation, GardenCare can assess the condition and recommend the right restoration work. After restoration, we can also discuss ongoing monthly garden maintenance.
1

Once-Off Lawn Restoration

This service is designed for lawns that are tired, patchy, weak or in need of revitalisation.

Our restoration service may include:
  • Lawn preparation and cleaning
  • Weed removal
  • Organic manure/compost lawn dressing
  • Lawn revitalisation
  • Treatment of problem areas
  • General finishing and clean-up

The aim is to give your lawn the care it needs to recover and develop a healthier, better-looking appearance.

2

Monthly Garden Maintenance

After restoration, we can continue looking after your garden on a monthly basis.

Our maintenance service can include:
  • Grass cutting
  • Lawn edging
  • Weed removal
  • General garden clean-up
  • Basic trimming and pruning
  • Keeping the lawn and garden neat and presentable

This allows us to maintain the work consistently rather than waiting until the garden becomes overgrown again.

💡

Our Recommendation

If your lawn currently needs significant attention, we would first recommend a once-off restoration. We can then discuss a monthly maintenance plan to keep it looking its best.
