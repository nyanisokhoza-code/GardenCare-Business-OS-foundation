# GardenCare Business OS v0.8.8

**Build:** Technical Costing / Break-even

Added an internal costing workspace for GardenCare.

## New
- Maximum 3-person crew costing.
- Internal worker cost per hour separated from customer-facing pricing.
- Director/owner job-time cost.
- Fuel calculated in litres per 100 km.
- Vehicle wear-and-tear cost per km separated from fuel.
- Equipment, consumables, waste/disposal and other direct costs.
- Monthly fixed overhead allocation per expected job.
- Monthly director/owner cost allocation.
- Break-even/minimum recovery calculation.
- Target selling price calculated from gross margin.
- Internal GardenCare restoration protocol checklist.
- Signature product categories retained in the local database.

Existing v0.8.7 QR/mobile flyer, quoting, invoicing, payment and customer workflows are preserved.
