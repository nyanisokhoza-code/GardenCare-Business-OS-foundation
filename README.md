# GardenCare Business OS v0.8.8

Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

This build preserves the v0.8.7 QR/mobile flyer system and adds **Costing / Break-even** to the left navigation. The costing page is internal-only and is designed to answer: what does this job truly cost, what is the break-even amount, and what price is required to achieve a chosen gross margin?

The vehicle model treats fuel as **L/100 km**. Example: 100 km at 12 L/100 km uses 12 L; at R30/L the fuel cost is R360. Vehicle wear/maintenance is entered separately as R/km.

Crew size is capped at 3. Monthly business costs are allocated per expected job rather than being hidden inside a simple percentage.

Use **Settings** or **Costing / Break-even → Save costing assumptions** to store the business assumptions used by the calculator.
